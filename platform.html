Edge Processor

The Edge Processor Accelerator is a data processing solution that works at the edge of your network to filter, mask, and transform your data close to its source before routing the processed data to your Splunk platform and external environments.

By paring down and sanitizing data before sending it out to Splunk indexes or Amazon S3 buckets, you can reduce data storage costs and help prevent confidential data from leaving your network. The Edge Processor Accelerator offering is a service to help you rapidly deploy and configure Edge Processors for different use cases within your environment.
